📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

• Desktop: Use Left & Right Arrow keys or [A] / [D] keys to move your basket.
• Touch / Mobile: Drag your finger across the screen or tap the left/right sides to steer!

⭐ OBJECTIVE & ITEMS
  • Yellow Star: +100 Points
  • Rainbow Star: +300 Points & Star Frenzy!
  • Shield Orb: Protects you from 1 Bomb strike
  • Magnet Orb: Attracts nearby stars to your basket
  • Bomb: Explodes! 3 Strikes = Game Over in Classic mode.
🔥 COMBOS & MULTIPLIERS

Catch stars consecutively without missing to raise your Combo Multiplier and multiply your catch scores!

ℹ️ ABOUT THE GAME

Catch the Falling Stars is a charming, vibrant arcade experience designed for players of all ages! Inspired by nostalgic classic arcade catchers, it brings physics, magical power-ups, and cheerful sound synthesis together.

Core Loop: Pilot your starry basket through celestial starfalls, gather rare cosmic gems, unlock achievements, and climb the high score leaderboards!
