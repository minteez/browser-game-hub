HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

• Desktop: Press ← and → Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to steer your starship.

• Mobile / Touch: Use the glowing on-screen arrow buttons at the bottom.

SHIELD POWER-UP
🛡️

Collect floating blue shields! The shield wraps around your ship and completely absorbs 1 asteroid collision, saving your ship!

DYNAMIC SPEED & SCORING

• Asteroids fall faster as your score grows higher!

• Dodge close to asteroids to increase your Combo Multiplier!

ABOUT THE GAME

Cosmic Dodge is an arcade space action dodging game designed for fast-paced fun. Navigate through an ever-densifying asteroid field while collecting energetic plasma shields.

Featuring procedural synthwave audio, responsive controls, smooth physics, and dynamic difficulty scaling to test your space piloting skills!
