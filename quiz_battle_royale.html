How To Play

1

Answer 10 Football Questions

Each match consists of 10 exciting multiple-choice questions about World Cups, legendary players, famous clubs, and match rules.

2

Build Streak Multipliers

Answering consecutively correct builds your Hot Streak multiplier! Fast answers grant extra time bonus points.

3

Use Strategic Lifelines

Stuck on a tricky question? Use the 50:50 Lifeline to remove two incorrect options or +15s Extra Time to think!

About Quiz Battle Royale

Quiz Battle Royale is an arcade football trivia game specifically designed for students aged 10-14. It combines stadium stadium atmosphere, fun trivia challenges, and fast-paced streak scoring mechanics!

Key Highlights:

  • 10 curated high-energy football trivia questions
  • Interactive jokers & streak combo multipliers
  • Multiple game modes: Classic, Speed Run & Sudden Death
  • Full accessibility support and offline local data saving
