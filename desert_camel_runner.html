About The Game

Concept

Desert Camel Runner: Oasis Dash is an inspired-by, reimagined endless-runner arcade game where players guide a brave camel across vibrant golden desert dunes, dodging cacti obstacles and airborne eagles.

Designed for players aged 10-14 and arcade enthusiasts, it combines classic side-scrolling mechanics with dynamic physics, aerial double-jumping, procedural desert dynamic environments, and dynamic synthesized audio loops.

Objective: Surpass your personal best distance, master timing double jumps, and unlock all desert achievements!
