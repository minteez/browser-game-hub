ABOUT NEON PADDLE

Futuristic Arcade Reimagined

Neon Paddle is an adrenaline-fueled, futuristic reimagining of classic breakout and paddle-and-ball arcade experiences. Designed for instant action, quick reflexes, and satisfying visual spectacles.

Core Objective: Control your high-velocity energy paddle at the base of the arena, keeping the plasma ball in continuous play while obliterating complex quantum block formations, dodging hazards, and collecting game-changing power-ups.

Inspired by legendary 80s arcade block-breakers, Neon Paddle introduces modern shaders, particle physics, combo multipliers, laser blasters, and multi-ball madness!

HOW TO PLAY

Master the energy paddle

CONTROLS

Mouse move, Touch drag, or A/D or Left/Right Arrow keys to steer paddle. Spacebar or Tap to release ball / fire lasers.

POWER-UPS
  • Wide Paddle: Expands your paddle width.
  • Laser Blasters: Equips paddle with rapid-fire lasers.
  • Multi-Ball: Splits energy balls into three.
  • Slow-Mo: Slows down ball speed for precision control.
HAZARDS & BLOCKS

Standard blocks break in 1 hit. Reinforced blocks require 2 hits. Indestructible blocks cannot be broken. Explosive blocks detonate adjacent blocks!
