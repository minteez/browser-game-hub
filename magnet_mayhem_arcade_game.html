How To Play

🎯 Objective: Survive magnetic obstacle courses, collect Glowing Energy Cores, avoid deadly traps/enemies, and defeat giant Bosses!

🧲 Magnetism Laws:

  • SAME Polarity (+ / + or - / -) ➔ REPEL (Pushes you away)
  • OPPOSITE Polarity (+ / -) ➔ ATTRACT (Pulls you closer)

⌨️ Keyboard Controls:

  • Move: Arrow Keys / WASD
  • Switch Polarity (+ / -): SPACEBAR
  • Dash Burst: SHIFT
  • Pause: ESC

📱 Mobile Controls: Use the virtual Joystick to steer and on-screen buttons to Switch Polarity & Dash.
