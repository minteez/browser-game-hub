ℹ️ ABOUT THE GAME

Concept & Theme: Oasis Mole Mayhem is a desert-themed arcade reaction game designed for players aged 8-12. Set in a lush desert oasis surrounded by golden sand dunes, palm trees, and cool turquoise water springs.

Gameplay Loop: Cheeky oasis critters pop up randomly across a 3x3 grid. Players test their reflexes by clicking or tapping the moles within 30 seconds while avoiding prickly cacti hazards!

Inspiration: Inspired by classic arcade Whack-a-Mole games, recreated with vibrant vector art, procedural Web Audio synth music, combo dynamics, and full accessibility options.
