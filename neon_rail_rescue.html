About Neon Rail Rescue

Neon Rail Rescue is a high-speed 2D arcade railway management game set in an advanced futuristic neon megacity.

The Concept: Rather than driving a single train, you are the Chief Railway Infrastructure Controller. You control yellow track switches, operate green/red signal lights, and clear safe pathways before high-speed trains arrive.

Gameplay Loop: Constantly observe incoming trains, predict track paths, toggle switches and signals, react to emergency slowdowns, and guide passenger trains to matching color terminals without crashing.

Mission: Prevent derailments, build combo multipliers, deliver passengers safely, and survive as the railway network continuously accelerates!
