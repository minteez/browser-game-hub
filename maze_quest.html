About The Game

Maze Quest 2026 is a fast-paced, arcade-style maze navigation game designed for players aged 9-13 and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages.

Inspired by classic 80s arcade maze runners, it features dynamic procedural maze generation, responsive keyboard & touch controls, collectible items, and dark Fog of War exploration.

Every generated maze is guaranteed to be 100% solvable with unique path combinations every single round!
