HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls:
  • Left / Right Arrows (A / D): Run forward and backward
  • Spacebar / Up Arrow (W): Jump over spikes & abysses
  • Mobile: On-screen touch D-Pad and Jump button
🎯 Objective:

Traverse through 6 dangerous obstacles, gather energy orbs for bonus points, and touch the Finish Flag to unleash your Superpower Victory Move!

⚠️ Hazards & Boosts:

Red Lasers/Spikes: Resets your position to safety!

Spring Pads: Launches HyperHero super high into the air!

ABOUT THE GAME

HyperHero: City Saver is a retro-inspired fast-paced 2D superhero side-scrolling platformer created for players who love classic arcade action!

Guide HyperHero across urban skyscrapers, jump over high-voltage lasers and acid gaps, collect energy orbs to power up, and reach the finish flag to activate an epic heroic superpower move!
