❓ How to Play

1️⃣

Tap or Click Cards: Click any card on the 4x4 grid to flip it face-up and reveal its emoji.

2️⃣

Find Matches: Tap a second card. If the emojis match, they stay face up!

3️⃣

Remember Locations: If they don't match, remember their positions and try again.

4️⃣

Build Combos: Matching cards consecutively without missing grants extra Combo points!

📖 About Emoji Match

Emoji Memory Match is an arcade brain-training game built specifically for kids aged 8–12!

Featuring vibrant emoji art, responsive Web Audio synth effects, customizable card themes, and accessible controls, it offers a fun and active way to sharpen focus and visual memory.

Designed as a modern reimagining of classic memory card concentration games.
