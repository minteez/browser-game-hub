HOW TO PLAY CLOCKWORK CLASH

1. Objective & Goal

You control a mechanical hero trapped inside a colossal celestial clock. Your goal in each section is to collect all 5 glowing Clock Fragments and reach the Exit Door before time runs out or your health is depleted.

2. Controls

Move Left / Right: A / D or Left/Right Arrows
Jump / Double Jump: W, Space, or Up Arrow

3. Hazards to Avoid

  • Rotating Gears: Sharp brass blades spinning rapidly. Touching them damages your core.
  • Pistons: Heavy mechanical stamps rhythmically slamming down. Time your dash beneath them!
  • Pendulums: Heavy weights swinging back and forth across platforms.
  • Bottom Abyss: Falling off the platform structure causes instant core failure.

4. Boosts & Scoring

  • Spring Launchers: Step on glowing pads to launch high into the upper levels.
  • Conveyor Belts: Moving belts that push you left or right; use them to gain extra momentum.
  • Combo Multiplier: Collecting fragments consecutively without taking damage multiplies your score!
