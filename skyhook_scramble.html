HOW TO PLAY

Master the cybernetic grapple hook to conquer the skies.

1
Grapple & Swing Click/Tap on any anchor point (pulsing blue nodes) or press Space / Click to attach your hook. Swing around anchors with momentum.
2
Momentum & Launch Use Left/Right arrows or A/D keys (or onscreen buttons) to thrust while swinging. Release grapple to launch yourself forward at high speed!
3
Collect Rings & Combos Fly through cyan energy rings to build your score multiplier. Collect power-ups for shields, magnet boosts, and speed surges!
4
Avoid Hazards Watch out for flying drones, moving platforms, breakable glass platforms, and deadly red laser barriers!

ABOUT THE GAME

Concept, inspiration, and development details.

Concept: Skyhook Scramble is a high-octane 2D arcade platformer built around momentum-based physics and precision grappling mechanics. Set in a futuristic cyber-sky filled with drifting platforms, kinetic wind streams, and energy anomalies, players must use fluid pendulum physics to ascend endlessly.

Inspiration: Inspired by classic arcade swinging games and modern momentum platformers, Skyhook Scramble captures the pure rush of high-speed aerial acrobatics combined with satisfying combo multipliers and procedural level generation.
