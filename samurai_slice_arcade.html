HOW TO PLAY

Master the Blade

CORE OBJECTIVE

Defend your dojo by slicing flying fruits, bamboo, and targets as they arc across the screen. Avoid explosive red bombs at all costs!

CONTROLS

  • Mouse / Touch: Click and drag/swipe quickly across targets to leave glowing katana slashes.
  • Keyboard Slashes: Use A (Left), D (Right), W (Up), S (Down), and Space (360° Sweep).
  • Blade Focus [Q]: Fill your focus meter to trigger slow-motion time precision.

SCORING & COMBOS

Slice multiple objects in a single continuous swipe to trigger high-multiplier combos and critical sparks for bonus points.

ABOUT THE GAME

Concept & Inspiration

RECREATED EXPERIENCE

Samurai Slice is a recreated, reimagined-style arcade tribute to the popular mobile classic Fruit Ninja. Built from scratch with custom 2D canvas physics, Web Audio procedural sound generation, and traditional Japanese sumi-e ink aesthetics, it brings high-speed slicing action directly to your browser.

UNIQUE FEATURES

Featuring tactical armored targets, rare explosive bombs, slow-motion Blade Focus ability, screen shake impact feedback, comprehensive career statistics, and local arcade high score tracking.
