HOW TO PLAY MANUAL

Automatic Forward Flight: Your hovercraft accelerates automatically through an endless procedural tunnel.
Controls: Use A / D, Arrow Keys, or Screen Taps to switch between the 5 lanes. Hold Shift / Space for Hyper Boost.
Hazards & Scoring: Dodge red barriers and laser gates. Pass close to obstacles to trigger Near-Miss combo multipliers. Collect yellow energy cells to boost your score and refuel.

ABOUT THE GAME

Turbo Tunnel is a hyper-velocity futuristic hover racing simulator inspired by classic 90s sci-fi arcade tube racers.

Designed for smooth 60 FPS performance, it features procedural tunnel curves, dynamic synthesised audio, and intense neon aesthetics.
