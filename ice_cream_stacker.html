About The Game

🍦 The Concept

Ice Cream Stacker is an arcade physics stacking game set in a bright, dessert-themed world! Your objective is simple yet addictive: stack scoops of ice cream as tall as possible on top of a waffle cone without letting the tower tumble into disaster.

🎮 How to Play

  • Drop Scoops: Tap anywhere on the screen, or press Space / Enter to release swinging scoops.
  • Time Your Drops: Precision is key! Land scoops dead-center on top of each other to earn PERFECT drops, bonus points, and build combo multipliers.
  • Balance Physics: Off-center scoops increase wobble and cause your stack to lean. If the total center of mass sways too far, your entire tower will collapse!
  • Watch the Wind: As your tower grows taller, gusts of wind will push dropping scoops sideways.
  • Pause Game: Press P or Esc or tap ⏸ PAUSE to pause the game at any time.

⭐ Features & Progression

  • Multiple Game Modes: Classic, Zen, Time Attack, and Hardcore.
  • Custom Cones: Score points to unlock waffle cone designs in the Cones Shop.
  • 50 Badges: Unlock achievements for high scores, precision streaks, and stacking heights.
