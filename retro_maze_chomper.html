ABOUT THE GAME

THE CONCEPT

Retro Maze Chomper is an original, neon-infused homage to classic 1980s arcade maze chasers. Built from scratch with smooth 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas graphics, custom Web Audio synth sound, and zero heavy external frameworks.

THE LORE & GAMEPLAY

Take control of the Orb Runner—a high-energy sentient sphere trapped inside a grid-locked neon matrix. Your goal is to devour all glowing Energy Pellets while outsmarting four autonomous Enemy Drones, each driven by distinct tactical tracking algorithms.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

  • Dynamic Enemy AI: Aggressive chase, interception prediction, random patrol, and scatter routines.
  • Power Mode & Combos: Turn the tables on drones and stack exponential multiplier bonuses (200, 400, 800, 1600).
  • Web Audio Synthesizer: Real-time procedural retro sound effects and background synth melodies.
  • Accessibility & Mobile: Swipe gestures, touch D-Pad, difficulty tuning, CRT filter toggle, and reduced flashing modes.

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Guide the glowing Orb Runner through the maze, devour all Energy Pellets, and evade the lethal Enemy Drones!

CONTROLS

Desktop Keyboard: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to navigate.

Mobile / Touch: Use the on-screen D-Pad or swipe directly anywhere across the screen.

POWER ORBS & DRONES

Eat large flashing corner Power Orbs to trigger Power Mode for 8 seconds. Drones turn vulnerable—capture them for bonus points (200, 400, 800, 1600)!

ENEMY DRONE AI TYPES

  • Red Drone: Directly chases your position.
  • Blue Drone: Intercepts by predicting where you move.
  • Green Drone: Patrols randomly until close by.
  • Purple Drone: Alternates between chasing and retreating.
