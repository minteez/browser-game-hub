HOW TO PLAY

Master the Sonar Radar & find the chest!

1

Click Any Tile to Dig

You begin with 5 guesses on a 5x5 grid map. Tap any tile on the island to start scanning.

2

Listen to Warmer / Colder Radar

After each dig, your radar tells you if you got 🔥 Warmer (closer to the gold) or ❄️ Colder (further away)!

Scorching: 1 tile away!
Warm: 2 tiles away
Cool: 3 tiles away
Freezing: 4+ tiles away
3

Uncover the Gold!

Find the chest before your digs run out to score massive points and keep your win streak alive!

ABOUT THE GAME

Discover the story behind Pirate's Gold Radar.

Pirate's Gold: Treasure Radar is an inspired-by, reimagined classic grid deduction puzzle created specifically for players aged 9–13 and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages.

Set on a mysterious tropical island, players use an advanced sonar detector to zero in on hidden pirate gold chests by interpreting dynamic distance signals and temperature feedback.

Pro Tip: Try the 7x7 Explorer or 9x9 Master grid modes in Game Modes when 5x5 becomes too easy!
